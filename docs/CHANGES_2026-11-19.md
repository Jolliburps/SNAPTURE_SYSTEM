## Camera and recognition

The existing local OWL-ViT detector and seven-category MobileNetV2 classifier are retained. No model retraining, weight replacement or confidence-policy relaxation is included.

- Added conditional low-resolution and possible overexposure checks; plain white paper is not automatically treated as overexposure.
- Standardized EXIF orientation and RGB conversion at detector and classifier entry points. Model-specific resizing and normalization remain unchanged.
- Stored accepted detector localization separately from the padded classification crop. The UI draws a green box only for valid accepted localization coordinates.
- Removed an extra selection tap for the single-item material-check flow. Multiple-item selection remains available where already supported.
- Updated network failure wording to cover both USB and wireless debugging.

See [Camera Phase 1](../PHASE1_CAMERA.md) for architecture and preprocessing details.

## Condition and preparation flow

1. Identify the supported category; preserve existing uncertainty and confirmation policies.
2. Ask a condition question with wording appropriate to that category.
3. Clean, dry items can show available suitable ideas, with an option to change condition.
4. Items needing preparation show material-specific instructions. Paper/cardboard drying does not use bottle-washing instructions.
5. Preparation finished opens one readiness question. Only a successful save updates the same scan to clean/dry; failed requests preserve the readiness screen for retry.
6. Not ready returns to preparation. Change condition returns to item details.
7. Mold, chemical contamination, unknown condition and generic damage receive an explanation rather than a generic bin message.

Material identity is independent of condition. Preparation does not clear the recorded previous contents or certify safety. Plastic rinsing requires known food/drink contents; treatment history still matters for wood.

## Torn material and project fit

- Clean, dry torn/crumpled paper has a distinct condition and offers paper collage/mosaic rather than projects requiring a whole sheet.
- Clean, dry usable cardboard scraps offer craft templates; fabric scraps offer cleaning cloth/patchwork choices.
- Generic cracked containers, sharp shell pieces and splintered wood are not automatically unlocked for reuse.
- Wet, needs-cleaning and unknown-condition records do not receive educational project suggestions prematurely.
- Choosing a new idea opens a project-fit check for usable size/shape, amount and tools. Users may choose another idea. Opening an existing saved project retains its progress.
- Size/quantity checks are user-reported practical checks, not camera measurements or numerically calibrated project requirements. The backend does not independently verify those dimensions.
- Historical assessments retain their saved snapshot. Existing scans may require an explicit condition update to receive the new suggestions.

## Later November 19 update - exposure, suitability and category explanations

This section supersedes the earlier project-fit checklist description above.

### Implemented
- Added the cause-of-wetness/dirt follow-up for all seven categories. Allowed preparation depends on material and reported exposure.
- Preserved exposure history after preparation. Drying does not clear chemical, grease, floodwater/sewage/animal-waste or unknown exposure restrictions.
- Restricted previously wet paper/cardboard to piece-based catalog projects.
- Added usable dimensions in cm, container capacity in ml, usable piece/container count and intact-versus-pieces form to the existing saved assessment answers.
- Filtered catalog ideas by condition, size, count, form and exposure. Server-side start/change project choices apply current suitability; undefined size specifications do not qualify.
- Removed tool-availability confirmation. Project cards list the required tools and materials instead.
- Added no-match guidance and a route to correct usable-material details. History restores saved size/count without borrowing another scan's answers.
- Withheld the generic wood shelf pending a load/construction specification.
- Added conservative edge-based paper/cardboard outline refinement: only an accepted detection with a clear overlapping quadrilateral boundary can expand. Other materials and uncertain/no-edge images keep the measured original box. Classification uses the refined region. Existing saved scans are not rewritten.
- Corrected scene focus-box presentation to use SceneItem coordinates for the displayed scene photo.
- Added shared explanations for all seven categories in material choices, confirmation, result and advice cards. PETE/HDPE explanations include full names, codes 1/2 and examples; category identifiers and classification are unchanged.

### Latest verification before push
- 35 targeted Django tests passed: exposure rules, project suitability, catalog filtering, preparation/history persistence, sheet extent refinement and single-item framing.
- 34 focused Android tests passed: condition profiles, preparation rules/readiness and fit helper behavior.
- TypeScript check passed.
- These runs are focused regression checks, not a new full-suite pass. The earlier full run's 20 failures remain unresolved as a group; no claim is made that this update fixes them all.
- Wireless phone connection and server operation were checked. Final visual acceptance of the complete flow, explanations and real-photo outline accuracy remains outstanding.

### Limits and review requirements
- Project dimension minima are prototype design assumptions, not CESD-approved specifications or guarantees of strength, safety or durability. Measurements/counts are user reports, not camera measurements. Uniform-size batches are assumed; mixed-size batches must be assessed separately.
- Gift-wrap minima do not establish fit around an arbitrary gift; project-specific target dimensions still need review.
- No model weights, confidence thresholds or datasets were changed or retrained by this update.
- No new real-photo accuracy measurement was performed. The outline refinement has synthetic regression coverage and needs real-phone evaluation.
- No database migration. Dependencies/build outputs, local logs, account data and credentials are not added in this push.

See [Material suitability update](SUITABILITY_UPDATE.md) for suitability limitations.
